# 2026-10-17: The seedling mark

The site's mark changed from a leaf with branching veins to a seedling (#102). Six rounds of
candidates were drawn as hand-written SVGs and judged on rendered sheets; the owner chose
after each round. The working files stay outside the repository; the chosen files are in
`docs/site/assets/images/`.

## Current State

- **The mark.** A seedling: a big leaf leaning right, split along an S-shaped midrib into a
  lit and a shaded tone, with tapered negative-space veins in a regular pinnate rhythm, each
  ending in one small round terminal (the only nod to a syntax tree); a small leaf low on the
  left; a tapered stem standing on a faint soil line inside a soft ground shadow.
- **Three forms.** `chloro-hero.svg` (and `-dark`) on the home page carries the effects: the
  ground shadow, a sheen on each leaf half, shading where the leaves meet the stem, and a thin
  highlight on the lit edge, all gradients and masks with no SVG filters, so they render the
  same everywhere. `chloro-small.svg` (and `-dark`) in the header is flat, without veins.
  `chloro.svg`, the favicon, is the small mark with `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)`
  inside the file.
- **Dark scheme.** The hero swaps files with the theme's `#only-light`/`#only-dark`
  fragments; the header logo is swapped by a CSS `content: url(...)` rule, so it follows the
  site's toggle; the favicon follows the browser's preference.
- **How candidates were judged.** Each round's sheet showed every candidate at 132 px on
  white, the wash (#eef7f0) and the dark surface (#17241c); at 32, 24 and 16 px at actual
  pixels; the 16 px render enlarged four times, pixelated and smoothed; a solid silhouette;
  and an 8 px Gaussian blur; plus a 24 px header mock beside the word and a browser tab strip.
  The blur and silhouette columns decided most questions of legibility at small sizes.
- **The rounds.**
  1. Six refinements of the old leaf (a cleanup, a binary tree, a monoline, chevron veins, a
     code minimap, mirrored halves). The critique of the old mark: lopsided, a midrib off the
     axis, veins at unrelated angles, five stroke weights, a stem lost on the dark surface,
     noise at 16 px.
  2. Nine sprouts. The owner liked s2 (one big and one small leaf, asymmetric); mirrored
     seed leaves read as a whale's tail going under water.
  3. Hybrids of round 1's cleanup with s2, and curvier joins between the leaves. Rejected as
     a direction.
  4. Refined s2/h1 seedlings with a botanical outline, tapered stem, two tones. R2 (two
     tones) was the fallback; R3 and R5 (with veins, R5 with the soil line) were preferred.
  5. Redrawn veins for R3 and R5: the first veins were hairlines ending in blobs and looked
     "janky". The chosen treatment (t4) has tapered wedges at angles that tighten toward the
     tip, filleted joins and ends on an inset of the edge, with uniform terminals.
  6. Effects on r5-t4, each judged alone: the ground shadow with the line kept, the sheen, the
     contact shading (filter-free, from offset leaf shapes; matches `feDropShadow` up close)
     and the edge highlight were kept; beads on the terminals looked like rivets on dark and
     were dropped.
- **Colour.** With the brighter mark, the light scheme's deep green, ink, links and primary
  moved from #14532d and #1d6b3f to #04771c (5.7:1 on white), #047f22 on hover.

## Missing

- The mark's own shade tones still use #1d6b3f and #14532d, a little duller than the new
  page green.
- No PNG or ICO fallback for the favicon; browsers without SVG favicons show none.
